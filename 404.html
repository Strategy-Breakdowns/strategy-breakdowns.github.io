Filo Sandbox

Last run: never

What it is for. As we automate more of the newsletter operation, the risk stops being "did someone forget" and becomes "did the automation quietly not run". Filo is the second pair of eyes on that. It exists so a broken GitHub Action or a skill that silently failed gets noticed the next morning rather than the week a sponsor asks where their report is.

What it does today. Once a day it reads the Notion Automations List, scans the last 24 hours across Attio, Notion, Slack, beehiiv and Tally for anything that should have triggered an automation, then checks both directions: what should have fired and did not, and what fired that should not have. It posts one short table to #filo-sandbox with a forecast of tomorrow's expected workload, so each day's findings can be checked against yesterday's prediction.

What it deliberately does not do. Filo never does the work itself. It does not draft, send, or mark anything complete, and it never writes to a sponsor-facing surface. It only reports. Every run is logged below.
